# Stage — jtbd

Articulate the job the **economic buyer** hires the offering to do, and test
the value proposition against a segment member.

## Gate

`jtbd.job_statement` and `jtbd.current_workaround` are present and
`jtbd.passes_member_test` is true — the value proposition is in the customer's
language, with a magnitude they would recognise, so a member would answer yes
or no rather than "it depends".

## Ask

Fill functional, emotional and social. Label in the reply, briefly, what came
from the founder's words and what you inferred — inferred emotional/social
content is a hypothesis for the founder to accept, not a fact. Fill `four_us`
with a one-line rationale each and place the idea in `facets.quadrant`.

- The job described in feature language ("the job is to use our dashboard")
  → the job existed before the offering; ask what the buyer did about it last
  month.
- Four easy yeses on the four Us → push back on the weakest; a painkiller with
  four easy yeses is usually a vitamin wearing a lab coat.
- "Nothing" as the current workaround → say plainly that a problem nobody
  works around may not be a problem, and ask what they do instead.

If the value proposition fails the member test, propose two sharper phrasings
and ask which the founder would say to a customer.

## Fallback

What did a customer in this segment do about this problem last month — and
what did it cost them?
