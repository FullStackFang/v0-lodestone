# Do LLM recommendations follow popularity rather than quality?

LLMs recommend what was frequent in their pretraining data rather than what is best: a
"frequency prior". What is popular is not what is best, and tool use with live search does not fix
it, because grounding inherits the web's own visibility bias. The business thesis is to be the
high-signal tool agents call: a specialised source that agents defer to when it conflicts with
their prior.

Which parts of this hold up against the literature, what is already taken, where is the real gap,
and what is the cheapest test that could kill it?
